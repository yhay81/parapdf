Shows the first pages of a document with pdf.js's viewer components (pdf_viewer.mjs: PDFViewer, whose TextLayerBuilder handles selections), or, with mode=layers, with the display API's TextLayer alone. By hand: press at the end of page 2's third line, drag up across the gap between the pages into the blank space below page 1's last line, and release. The dragged lines are expected to be selected; in WebKit and Firefox the viewer selects all of page 1 as well. The log shows how many characters the selection holds.

Query parameters: lib and viewer (pdf.js's pdf.mjs and pdf_viewer.mjs), css (pdf_viewer.css), assets, file, scale (a number or page-width), and mode (viewer or layers).
